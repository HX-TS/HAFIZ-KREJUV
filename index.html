PTW SHIFT VALIDITY RULE
A PTW OR VEC MUST REMAIN VALID UNTIL THE END OF THE APPLICABLE SHIFT. IF THE PERMIT EXPIRES BEFORE 18:59 FOR DAY SHIFT OR BEFORE 06:59 FOR NIGHT SHIFT, IT CANNOT BE USED FOR THAT SHIFT. A NEW PERMIT SHOULD BE ACTIVATED FOR THAT SHIFT. THE PREVIOUS FULLY USABLE SHIFT WILL FLASH RED AS THE RENEWAL WARNING.
